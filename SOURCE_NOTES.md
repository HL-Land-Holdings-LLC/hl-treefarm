# Source and verification notes

- Main navigation: Home, About, Christmas, Weddings, Fishing, Landscaping, Cabin, Contact.
- Site description: a roughly 1,100-acre Appalachian valley used for Christmas trees, events, farming, and woodland stewardship.
- Christmas is a choose-and-cut family visit.
- Norway spruce has strong upward-slanting branches suited to heavy ornaments.
- Colorado blue spruce has a classic shape and blue-green/aquamarine foliage.
- A cut-tree care PDF existed on the old site.
- The Christmas page contained annual hours and pricing, demonstrating that these details need prominent seasonal updating.

## Corroborated by public Kentucky listings and regional coverage

- Christmas-tree production dates to the early 1980s.
- The farm has offered white pine, Norway spruce, Colorado blue spruce, and pre-cut Fraser fir.
- Historical offerings included wreaths, garland/greenery, tree shaking and baling, a shelter, and hayrides.
- Landscape inventory has included field-grown evergreen, shade, and flowering trees and shrubs.
- The property includes a private lake, trails, hills, fields, woodland, and an old barn used for events.
